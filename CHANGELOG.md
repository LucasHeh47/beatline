# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Recorded instruments:** 128 real instruments (pianos, guitars, bass guitars, strings, brass, choir, woodwinds, organs and more) from the FluidR3 Mono SoundFont, in the browser, the Add menu and the chord tool. Each is loudness-matched, and harder notes sound brighter and louder.
- **Recorded drum kits:** acoustic, rock, room, jazz and brush kits and real percussion, next to the electronic drums.
- **Templates sound real:** Pop, Lo-fi, Hip-hop, Rock band and Singer-songwriter now use piano, electric piano, bass guitars, organ and a real drum kit. The Rock band template starts with drums and bass straight away.
- **MIDI files keep their instruments:** imported tracks play the instrument the file names (drums on a drum kit), and exported patterns say which instrument each track is.
- **Recording keeps going:** the playhead used to stop at the end of the song (one bar into an empty one) while the recording carried on. Now it keeps moving, the take grows on its track with its waveform as you play, and the view follows it.
- **Metronome while recording:** clicks all the way through a take (on by default, in the Recording panel), not just during the count-in.
- **Count-in countdown** in the transport, and notes played on a MIDI keyboard keep how hard they were played.

## 0.1.0

The first version of Beatline: make beats, write songs and record your guitar.

- **Channel rack and step sequencer:** drums, synths and samples as rows; click or drag across steps to program a beat, with swing, pattern lengths of 1 to 8 bars, and ready-made beats in ten styles (house, rock, boom bap, trap, lo-fi, reggaeton, funk, drum & bass, disco, half-time).
- **Built-in sounds:** 24 drum and percussion sounds made by Beatline itself (808 and punchy kicks, snares, claps, hats, cymbals, toms, percussion, an 808 bass) and 14 synth presets (sub and Reese bass, leads, supersaw, pads, strings, electric piano, organ, bells, plucks, brass).
- **Piano roll:** draw, move, stretch and erase notes, velocity, ghost notes, the song's key shown as light rows, chord names, a chord stamp, and tools to quantize, humanize, legato, arpeggiate, strum, make chords and fit notes to the key.
- **Chord progressions:** pop, emotional, 50s, royal road, jazz, canon, dark pop, flamenco, trap and 12-bar blues in any key, held, stabbed or arpeggiated, with a bass line, and smooth voice leading.
- **Playlist:** arrange patterns and audio clips on tracks; move, copy, slice and trim them, fade audio, loop a section, and drop audio files straight in.
- **Mixer:** a track per instrument and audio track with meters, faders, pan, mute and solo, and effects: EQ, compressor, noise gate, limiter, reverb, tempo-synced delay, guitar amp with cabinet, distortion, chorus, phaser, filter and stereo width, plus ready-made chains for guitar, vocals, drums and the master.
- **Recording:** record guitar, bass or vocals from an audio interface or microphone, hear yourself through the amp and effects, with metronome, count-in, latency correction and takes when looping; a tuner for guitar (several tunings), bass and ukulele.
- **Playing live:** MIDI keyboards, and the computer keyboard as a piano; record what you play into the pattern.
- **Synth and sampler settings:** two oscillators with unison and FM, noise, filter with envelope, volume envelope, LFO, glide and mono; samplers with pitch, envelope, reverse and choke groups.
- **Files:** projects save automatically and are recovered after a crash; import WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A and AIFF, and MIDI files; sample folders in the browser; export WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG or FLAC, with loudness for streaming, and stems.
